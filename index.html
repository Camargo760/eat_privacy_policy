Overview

E.A.T. (Eats Around Town) is a mobile app that helps customers find and message local food vendors, and lets vendors broadcast their live location while selling. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have about it.

This policy applies to everyone who uses the E.A.T. app — customers, vendors, and administrators.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, and phone number when you create an account.
  • Vendor storefront details: if you apply to become a vendor, your business name, category, description, operating hours, and related profile details.
  • Messages: the content of chat messages sent between customers and vendors through the app.
  • Reports: if you report a vendor, the reason you provide and which vendor it concerns.
  • Location data: see the dedicated section below.
  • Basic device and usage information: standard technical data (such as connection and error logs) needed to operate the app reliably.

Location data

Vendors choose when to share their live location by tapping "Go Live" in the app. Nothing is broadcast before that action, and location sharing stops immediately when a vendor taps "Stop Sharing." While live, a vendor's device periodically sends an updated position so their marker moves on the customer map in near real time. We do not collect a vendor's location in the background or when the app is closed.

Customers' device location is used to center the map and sort nearby vendors by distance. This is used locally to improve what's shown to you and to find vendors near your device's location.

How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account, and to let you log in.
  • To operate core app features: showing nearby vendors, live location broadcasting, and in-app messaging.
  • To review and process vendor applications.
  • To review reports and moderate accounts that violate our guidelines, including suspending accounts.
  • To communicate with you about your account, such as confirming your email or responding to a report you filed.
  • To maintain the security and reliability of the app.

We do not use your information to serve ads, and we do not sell your personal information.

How information is shared

  • Between users: a vendor's profile, live location (while broadcasting), and messages are visible to the customers they interact with, and vice versa.
  • With our service providers: we use third-party infrastructure providers — including Supabase for authentication and data storage, and a map-tile provider for rendering the live map — to operate the app. These providers process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.
  • With administrators: our admin team can access reports, vendor applications, and account status strictly to moderate the platform and respond to reports.
  • For legal reasons: we may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or property of E.A.T., our users, or the public.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Data retention

We keep account information for as long as your account is active. Chat messages are kept so conversations persist across your devices. A vendor's live location is not retained once sharing stops — only the fact that a session occurred, and when. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us (see below).

Your choices

  • Vendors can stop sharing their location at any time by tapping "Stop Sharing."
  • You can edit your profile details from within the app.
  • You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us.
  • You can delete the app at any time to stop it from operating on your device.

Children's privacy

E.A.T. is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your region). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections between the app and our servers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.
